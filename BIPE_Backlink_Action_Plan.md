## 🥇 TIER 1 — Do these first (highest authority, biggest payoff)

### A. Get listed on the aggregators that already rank for JEECUP
These sites occupy the SERPs you can't beat head-on — so **join them.** A claimed
college profile gives you (1) a backlink, (2) referral traffic from students
already searching, and (3) inclusion in their "colleges accepting JEECUP" lists.
Most are free.

| Site | What to do | Add/claim page |
|---|---|---|
| **Careers360** | Add/claim college | careers360.com → "Add your college" |
| **Shiksha** | Add/claim college | shiksha.com → list your institute |
| **CollegeDunia** | Add/claim college | collegedunia.com → add college |
| **CollegeDekho** | Add/claim + lead form | collegedekho.com |
| **GetMyUni** | Add college | getmyuni.com |
| **Sarvgyan / IndCareer / CollegeSearch** | Add listing | sarvgyan.com · indcareer.com |

> **Priority within Tier 1.** These are where 27k/mo of "jeecup counselling"
> traffic actually goes. Being on the list beside government polytechnics is worth
> more than ranking #15 yourself.

### B. Verify the official .gov / .ac listings link to bipevns.org
High-authority, factually true, and often missing the website link:
- **AICTE** — `facilities.aicte-india.org` → find BIPE (Permanent ID **1-488233171**) → ensure website = bipevns.org.
- **BTEUP** — `bteup.ac.in` affiliated-colleges list → confirm BIPE / code **4455** entry + website.
- **AISHE** — `aishe.gov.in` institution record → confirm details + website.
- **JEECUP / UPDESCO** participating-institute directory → confirm code **4455** maps to bipevns.org.

## 🚫 What NOT to do (these get you penalized)

- ❌ Paid link schemes, PBNs, link farms, "5000 backlinks for ₹999" gigs
- ❌ Mass auto-directory blasts with inconsistent NAP
- ❌ Blog-comment / forum-signature spam
- ❌ Exact-match anchor-text stuffing

**Quality and relevance beat volume every time.** 20 real education/local
citations outperform 500 junk links — and won't risk a manual action.
