🎓 **A proud day for BIPE.**

We are honoured to welcome **Prof. (Dr.) S. P. Tewari** as **Director** of Banaras Institute of Polytechnic & Engineering, Varanasi.

For **38 years**, Prof. Tewari taught Mechanical Engineering at **IIT (BHU), Varanasi** — joining as a Lecturer in 1981 and retiring as Professor in 2019. Over that career he guided **8 doctoral scholars** and **40 M.Tech dissertations**, published **56 research papers**, and authored **5 books** on manufacturing and welding technology.

He brings with him:

🔹 Ph.D. in Welding — IT-BHU, Varanasi
🔹 Fellow of the Institution of Engineers (India), FIE
🔹 Member, **AICTE Expert Visit Committee**
🔹 Examiner & moderator for **UPSC and UPPSC** civil services examinations
🔹 Board of Studies member — KNIT Sultanpur, VBS Purvanchal University, SRM University Barabanki, IET Faizabad
🔹 Best Paper Award, ICSET 2018, **New York**

In his message to our students, he writes:

*"The best engineers I taught were never the ones with the highest marks. They were the ones who were not afraid of the workshop."*

That is exactly why we are glad he has chosen a polytechnic. Many of our students are the first engineers in their family — and they will now learn in an institution led by someone who spent a lifetime at one of India's finest engineering schools.

Welcome, Sir. 🙏

**Prof. (Dr.) S. P. Tewari — former IIT (BHU) Professor — joins BIPE as Director.**

BIPE Varanasi is honoured to welcome Prof. (Dr.) S. P. Tewari as Director.

Prof. Tewari taught Mechanical Engineering at IIT (BHU), Varanasi for 38 years, joining in 1981 and retiring as Professor in 2019. He holds a Ph.D. in Welding from IT-BHU and is a Fellow of the Institution of Engineers (India).

He has guided 8 doctoral scholars and 40 M.Tech dissertations, published 56 research papers and authored 5 books. He has served on AICTE Expert Visit Committees, as an examiner for UPSC and UPPSC, and on the Board of Studies at KNIT Sultanpur, VBS Purvanchal University, SRM University Barabanki and IET Faizabad.

Banaras Institute of Polytechnic & Engineering is an AICTE-approved, BTEUP-affiliated polytechnic at Phoolpur, Varanasi, offering three-year diplomas in Civil, Electrical, Mechanical (Production), Computer Science and Dairy Engineering.

Admissions are through JEECUP — institute code **4455**.
