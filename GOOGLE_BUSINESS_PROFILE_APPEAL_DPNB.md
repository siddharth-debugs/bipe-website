# Google Business Profile — appeal guide
**Routing ID: DPNB** · Banaras Institute of Polytechnic & Engineering
Post removed 5 Aug 2026 · Posting currently switched OFF for the profile

---

## What actually happened

Google removed the Director announcement post and **disabled posting for the whole Business Profile**.

**The cause was the phone number in the post body.** Google's Business Profile posts content policy treats contact details — phone numbers, email addresses, WhatsApp numbers — inside post *text* as a violation. They belong in the profile's own contact fields, where BIPE's already are. The post repeated `+91 91986 46464` at the end, which is what tripped it.

Nothing else in the post was a problem: the photograph, the "Learn more" button and the link to bipevns.org/director are all fine.

---

## Before you appeal — 3 minutes, do this first

An appeal is far more likely to succeed if the profile is clean when a reviewer looks at it.

1. **Check your other live posts** for phone numbers or email addresses in the text. Delete or edit any you find. If a reviewer opens the profile and sees the same violation sitting in another post, the appeal fails.
2. **Check the "from the business" description** and any Q&A answers you've written for the same thing.
3. Confirm the profile's phone number is correctly filled in the **contact fields** — that is where it belongs and it is almost certainly already there.

## How to appeal

Google moves this interface around, so use whichever of these you find first — all three reach the same review queue.

**Route 1 — from the email (easiest).**
Open the "Your post has been removed" email. Use the **Help Center / appeal** link inside it. Because you are arriving from the notification, the Routing ID is already attached to your case.

**Route 2 — from Business Profile Manager.**
Sign in as an **Owner or Manager** of the profile, open the profile, and look for the removed post or a policy notification. Where an **Appeal** or **Request review** option is offered, use it.

**Route 3 — Business Profile Help → Contact us.**
Go to Google Business Profile Help, choose **Contact us**, pick the topic covering posts or a policy/suspension issue, and quote the Routing ID in the first line of your message.

**Always quote:** `Routing ID: DPNB`

> Routing ID: DPNB
>
> Business: Banaras Institute of Polytechnic & Engineering, Gajokhar, Phoolpur, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh 221206, India.
>

>

>
> We have removed that post and have reviewed our remaining posts, our business description and our Q&A answers to confirm that none of them contain telephone numbers or email addresses. Our contact details remain only in the profile's own contact fields, where they belong.
>
> Our future posts will contain no contact information of any kind. The post in question was purely informational — announcing that a former Professor of Mechanical Engineering from IIT (BHU), Varanasi has joined our institute as Director — with a photograph and a "Learn more" link to the relevant page on our own website, https://bipevns.org/director.
>
> We would be grateful if posting could be restored to our profile. This is our admissions season and the profile is how many local families find and contact the college.
>
> Thank you for your time.

**Tone matters here.** Reviewers respond well to a short message that admits the specific breach, states the specific fix, and promises the specific future behaviour. Do not argue that the rule is unreasonable, and do not pad it with extra detail.

---

## What to expect

- Most appeals of this type are decided within **a few days**; some take up to two weeks.
- You will get a reply to the same email address that received the removal notice.
- If the appeal succeeds, posting returns and you can publish the corrected post immediately.

---

## Two things NOT to do

1. **Do not try to post again while the block is active.** Repeat attempts during an active restriction can escalate from a posting block to a profile-level suspension — which would take the whole listing out of Maps and Search during counselling season. That is a far worse outcome than losing one post.
2. **Do not submit the appeal repeatedly.** Multiple submissions push you to the back of the queue rather than the front. Submit once and wait.

## Standing rule for every future Google post

**No phone numbers. No email addresses. No WhatsApp numbers. No prices or discounts. No ALL-CAPS. No `**asterisks**` — they render literally.**

Contact details live in the profile fields. A post that repeats them gains nothing and risks the profile.
